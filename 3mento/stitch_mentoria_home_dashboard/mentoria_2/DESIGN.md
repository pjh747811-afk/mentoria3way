## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and approachable environment tailored for career mentorship, professional growth, and institutional public subsidy matchmaking. It fuses the structured professionalism required by institutional grant workflows with the warm, aspirational human connection central to 1-on-1 industry mentorship.

The aesthetic follows a **Corporate / Modern Minimalist** philosophy with tactile refinement:
- High typographic legibility optimized for bilingual Korean/English data density and alphanumeric scanning.
- Calm, high-trust chromatic layering dominated by deep maritime blues, active cobalt interaction points, and grounding institutional teals.
- Structured card architecture with soft elevation and clean borders that organize dense multi-stage grants, mentor credentials, and application metrics without cognitive overload.

## Colors

The palette balances public-sector credibility with high-conversion consumer tech vitality:

- **Primary Accent (`#2563EB`)**: An electric, focused cobalt blue reserved for decisive user actions, active states, key focus outlines, and primary progress indicators.
- **Secondary Navy (`#1B2A4A`)**: A deep institutional anchor tone used for top-level navigation shells, primary headlines, verified corporate badges, and commanding structural elements.
- **Tertiary Teal (`#0D9488`)**: Dedicated to grant eligibility confirmations, approved statuses, matching scores, verified mentor markers, and positive validation moments.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: A cool, clinical off-white canvas that eliminates eye fatigue during lengthy application reviews.
- **Card Surface & Border**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces framed by hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) to maintain clear structural divisions.
- **Text & Body Gradients**: Primary text sits at Slate 900 (`#0F172A`), supporting descriptions at Slate 600 (`#475569`), and subtle disabled metadata at Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

Noto Sans serves as the universal neo-grotesque driver for clean bilingual rendering across Korean scripts (Hangeul) and Latin characters.

- **Numerics & Metrics**: Numeric figures across match rates, monetary funding amounts (₩), and D-day deadlines leverage bold font weights (`700` and `800`) with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em`) to provide instant visual impact.
- **Editorial Hierarchy**: Display headers set the tone on landing portals, while `headline-sm` and `body-md` handle high-frequency data structures such as grant requirements, mentor bios, and timeline agendas.
- **Microcopy & Metadata**: Labels emphasize semi-bold and bold weights at 11–12px for badges, tag categories, and form helpers, preserving high legibility against tinted surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict breakpoint containment:

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12 columns with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters, max-width constrained to `1280px`, and outer canvas margins of `32px` (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and section margins of `24px` (`1.5rem`). Split-view layouts (e.g., mentor profile alongside session booking) stack into sequential cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `16px` gutters and `16px` margin padding. Grid-based cards transition to single-column full-width feeds.

Component spacing operates on a consistent 4px/8px modular rhythm:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge internals, input icon gaps, and status tags.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for item gaps in stacked lists and form fields.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px) for card padding, generating open, breathable surfaces that facilitate rapid scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs low-contrast outlines paired with ambient, diffused shadows to retain a clean, institutional finish:

- **Base Surface (Flat)**: White cards rest on the `#F8FAFC` background bordered with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` rule. No initial shadow is required for standard structural containers.
- **Interactive Card Elevation**: Resting interactive cards feature an ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Hover & Active Depth**: Upon cursor hover, cards subtly elevate with an expanded diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.04)` combined with an accent-tinted border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Floating Overlays & Menus**: Sticky reservation headers, date pickers, and filter modals utilize `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.05)` with pure white solid backgrounds to ensure complete content occlusion.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined curvature scale (`roundedness: 2` base) to balance friendly engagement with structural precision:

- **Cards & Modal Containers**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), providing generous, soft silhouettes that reduce visual harshness across data-dense boards.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Styled with standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to preserve a crisp, reliable utility feel.
- **Badges, Tags, & Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create clear differentiation between actionable inputs and informational status badges.
- **Avatars**: Circular (`rounded-full`) for individual mentor faces; `rounded-md` (6px) for public institution logos and grant sponsor emblems.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg` (8px), font weight `600`. Hover state darkens to `#1D4ED8` with a fast 150ms ease transition. Focus ring uses a 3px outline in `#93C5FD`.
- **Secondary (Institution)**: Deep navy `#1B2A4A` fill with white text for official grant applications or submission confirmations.
- **Outline / Ghost**: 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, `#1B2A4A` text, transparent background. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Status Pills
- **Match Score & Success**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F0FDFA` background, `#0D9488` text, and a fine border in `#CCFBF1`.
- **Government Grant Category**: Pill-shaped, `#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, displaying category titles (e.g., "청년도약", "R&D 바우처").
- **D-Day / Deadline Urgent**: `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, bold font styling (`label-sm`).

### Cards
- **Mentor Card**: Built on a `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `rounded-xl` (24px) corners. Internal padding is `space-lg` (24px). Features a top row with mentor avatar, name, and current enterprise badge, a center body with specialized mentoring tags, and a bottom row displaying review ratings and booking action triggers.
- **Grant Policy Card**: Displays the managing ministry emblem, program title, application deadline countdown pill, summary criteria bullets, and an estimated grant support amount highlighted in `metric-stat` font.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text & Select Fields**: Minimum height of 44px, `rounded-md` (8px), border in `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus changes border to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow. Placeholder styled in `#94A3B8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square/circle with a 1.5px `#CBD5E1` boundary. Checked state delivers a solid `#2563EB` fill housing a sharp white checkmark.

### Additional Specialized Components
- **Timeline / Application Stepper**: Connects multiple stages (Application, Document Review, Interview, Final Matching) using a 2px horizontal line alternating between `#E2E8F0` (pending) and `#0D9488` (completed), anchoring current milestones with active pulse rings.
- **Metric Highlight Box**: Inset container with `#F8FAFC` fill, `rounded-lg` borders, and centered metric data showcasing mentor response rates, cumulative grant funding awarded, and mentee placement success.

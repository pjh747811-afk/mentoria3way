# Design System Document

## Brand & Style
The brand identity is energetic, high-contrast, and futuristic, leveraging a dark color mode with vibrant neon accents to evoke a dynamic, digital-first experience.

## Colors
- **Color Mode:** Dark
- **Primary:** `#ff2d78` (Vibrant Pink)
- **Secondary:** `#00ffcc` (Electric Cyan)
- **Tertiary:** `#ffe04a` (Warm Yellow)
- **Neutral:** `#28283e` (Deep Dark Navy/Slate)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Sora
- **Body:** Inter
- **Labels:** Space Grotesk

## Layout & Spacing
Standard 1rem gutters and 1.5rem outer margins maintain consistent rhythm across responsive viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal surface layers paired with low-opacity glowing ambient shadows derived from the vibrant palette.

## Shapes
Soft roundedness (`1`) providing subtle, clean curves across all interactive surfaces.

## Components
Buttons, inputs, cards, and chips adhere to the soft rounded geometry and high-contrast dark theme styling.
